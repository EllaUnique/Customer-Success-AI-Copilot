# Customer Success AI Copilot: Sample Test Cases

These examples were created using fictional customer scenarios to test how the AI workflow handles different types of customer requests.

---

## Test Case 1: Frustrated Customer

### Customer Message

> "I've contacted support twice this week and still haven't received an answer. I'm really disappointed because I need this resolved today."

### AI Output

**Issue:** Unresolved support request

**Sentiment:** Very frustrated

**Priority:** High

**Recommended Action:** Review the previous requests, identify the reason for the delay, and escalate if necessary.

**Follow-Up:** Within 24 hours

**CRM Note:** Customer has contacted support twice without resolution and is highly frustrated. Immediate follow-up recommended.

---

## Test Case 2: New Customer Onboarding

### Customer Message

> "Hi, I just joined and I'm a little confused about how to get started. Is there someone who can walk me through the setup?"

### AI Output

**Issue:** Onboarding assistance

**Sentiment:** Neutral

**Priority:** Medium

**Recommended Action:** Provide onboarding resources and offer guidance through the initial setup.

**Follow-Up:** Within 1 to 2 business days

**CRM Note:** New customer requested assistance with initial setup.

## Test Case 3: Billing Concern

### Customer Message

> "I noticed a charge on my account that I don't recognize. Can someone please check what happened?"

### AI Output

**Issue:** Unrecognized billing charge

**Sentiment:** Concerned

**Priority:** High

**Recommended Action:** Review the customer's billing history and account details before providing an explanation.

**Follow-Up:** As soon as the account has been reviewed

**CRM Note:** Customer reported an unfamiliar charge. Billing review required.

## Test Case 4: Product Feedback

### Customer Message

> "I really like the product, but it would be much easier for me if I could export my reports directly to Excel."

### AI Output

**Issue:** Product feature request

**Sentiment:** Positive

**Priority:** Low

## Test Case 5: Potential Churn

### Customer Message

> "I'm considering cancelling because I haven't been getting enough value from the service. Is there anything you can do to help?"

### AI Output

**Issue:** Customer considering cancellation

**Sentiment:** Dissatisfied

**Priority:** High

**Recommended Action:** Route to a customer success representative for a personal conversation. Review the customer's usage and identify opportunities to provide additional value.

**Follow-Up:** Promptly

**CRM Note:** Customer is considering cancellation due to perceived lack of value. Customer success intervention recommended.

## What These Tests Demonstrate

These scenarios were selected to test different customer situations:

| Scenario            | Priority | Main AI Task            |
| ------------------- | -------- | ----------------------- |
| Frustrated customer | High     | Sentiment + escalation  |
| New customer        | Medium   | Onboarding              |
| Billing concern     | High     | Risk identification     |
| Product feedback    | Low      | Feedback classification |
| Potential churn     | High     | Retention opportunity   |

The goal is not for AI to make the final decision. The goal is to give a customer success representative useful information and a starting point for action.

## Testing Approach

For each scenario, I considered whether the AI could:

1. Understand the customer's main issue
2. Recognize the customer's emotional state
3. Identify the appropriate priority
4. Recommend a reasonable next step
5. Suggest an appropriate follow-up
6. Create a useful CRM note

I would continue testing the workflow with more complex and ambiguous customer conversations before using it in a real customer environment.
